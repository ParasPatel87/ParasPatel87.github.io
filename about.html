ABOUT

A computational lens
on the material world.

I am a Doctoral Student in Condensed Matter Physics in the Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, at The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, Vadodara, Gujarat, India.

My academic work focuses on first-principles and density functional theory (DFT) based investigations for functional applications. My research interests include the structural, electronic, mechanical, and vibrational properties of materials under extreme conditions; the design and optimization of low-dimensional materials, including 2D monolayers and nanoclusters, for energy applications; and high-energy-density materials and molecular solids.

I completed my M.Sc. in Physics, specializing in condensed matter physics, at MSU Baroda in 2022, following a B.Sc. in Physics in 2020. I have served as a Temporary Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics since 2022.
