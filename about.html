ABOUT

A computational lens
on the material world.

I am a Ph.D. Candidate and Senior Research Fellow (SRF) in condensed matter physics at The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, where I have also served four terms as a Temporary Assistant Professor.

My research uses first-principles density functional theory (DFT), crystal structure prediction, and machine-learning interatomic potentials to investigate materials—from two-dimensional monolayers and nanoclusters to high-energy-density molecular solids under extreme pressure. I am interested in connecting atomic-scale structure with electronic, mechanical, and vibrational behaviour, including lattice dynamics and electron–phonon coupling.
